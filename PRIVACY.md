# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 15, 2024

## Introduction

KodeKit ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web-based toolkit.

## Summary of Key Points

- **Zero Data Collection**: All processing happens locally in your browser
- **No Personal Information**: We don't collect or store any personal data
- **No Tracking**: We don't use cookies or tracking technologies
- **Client-Side Processing**: Your files never leave your device
- **Open Source**: Our code is transparent and available for review

## Information We Don't Collect

KodeKit is designed with privacy as a core principle. We specifically **do not**:

- Collect or store any personal information
- Track your usage or behavior
- Use cookies for tracking purposes
- Store any files you process using our tools
- Maintain user accounts or profiles
- Process payment information
- Share data with third parties for marketing purposes
- Collect analytics beyond anonymous usage statistics

## How Our Tools Work

All file processing and conversions happen directly in your browser:

- Files are processed locally using browser-based technologies (JavaScript)
- No data is transmitted to our servers
- Your files never leave your device
- All operations are performed client-side
- No data persistence between sessions

## Technical Implementation

Our privacy-first approach is implemented through:

- **Client-Side Processing**: All computations run in your browser's JavaScript engine
- **No Backend Storage**: We don't maintain databases or server-side storage
- **Local Storage Only**: Any saved preferences use your browser's local storage
- **No Authentication**: No accounts means no personal data collection
- **No APIs**: We don't use APIs that would transmit your data

## Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data:

- All tools operate entirely in your browser
- No data persistence between sessions
- No server-side storage
- All processing is done locally
- HTTPS encryption for all web traffic
- Regular security audits of our codebase

## Your Rights

Under various privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have the right to:

- **Access**: Request copies of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data
- **Restriction**: Request limitation of processing
- **Objection**: Object to processing of your data
- **Portability**: Request transfer of your data
- **Withdraw Consent**: Revoke previously given consent

Since we don't collect personal data, these rights primarily apply to any information that might be collected by third-party services. To exercise these rights regarding third-party services, please contact them directly.

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

## International Users

By using our service, you consent to the transfer of any information to your country or region, regardless of data protection laws. However, since we don't collect personal data, international data transfer concerns are minimal.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

- Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
- Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
- Providing a notification on our website

We recommend reviewing this policy periodically for any changes.

## Data Breach Procedures

In the unlikely event of a data breach:

1. We will assess the scope and impact
2. We will notify affected users within 72 hours
3. We will work with relevant authorities as required

Since we don't store personal data, the risk of a significant data breach is minimal.
